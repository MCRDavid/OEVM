Data sources and transparency

Last reviewed: 2026-10-23. This page lists every operator source this project uses or plans to use, how each one publishes its data, the rate limits that apply, and what has been found while reading the data. It reports what was observed and when. It does not say whether anyone has met their legal duties.

Sources

OCPI 2.2.1 says a location whose publish flag is false may not be shown on a website or app, so those are never shown. Locations with no flag are not shown either, unless the repository owner has recorded a decision for that operator, with the reason and date below. A flag set to false is always respected.

Rate limits

The Public Charge Point Regulations 2023 set no limit on how often data users may request the data. Regulation 10(5) requires the data to be available free of charge without terms on its use. DfT's guidance allows terms covering the means of access, which is where operators' rate limits come from.

  • “terms and conditions covering the means of access to the data and API(s) are permissible” (DfT, Public Charge Point Regulations 2023 guidance)

This project never sends more than one request per second to any operator's host, keeps within every limit an operator, or the company hosting its data, publishes with a safety margin on top, and waits as long as a server asks before contacting it again. Gaps are measured from the end of the previous request and shared by every operator on the same host. Automated tests check these rules.

VAT in prices

OCPI 2.2.1 prices exclude VAT, and each price can give the VAT rate that applies to it. Where every price in a tariff gives a rate, this project adds it and shows the price including VAT. A price with no rate is read by OCPI as having no VAT, but VAT on public charging in the UK is at the standard rate of 20%, so such a price is likely to be lower than what a driver pays.

In the daily run of 22 October 2026, 10 of the 12 operators fetched gave a VAT rate on their prices, and char.gy and Clenergy EV gave none. char.gy's own pricing page gives each of its prices 20% higher than its feed does. Clenergy EV's website gives no charging prices, but 120 of the 126 prices per kWh in its feed come to a whole penny once 20% is added. The details are in each operator's findings above.

The repository owner decided on 22 October 2026 that prices on the map should all include VAT, so they can be compared. For each operator listed here, VAT at the rate shown is added to every price that gives no rate, and the price says this project added it. A price with no rate from any other operator is shown as published and marked "VAT not stated". The tariffs themselves are published unchanged.

  • char.gy: 20%, decided 2026-10-22 (evidence)
  • Clenergy EV: 20%, decided 2026-10-22 (evidence)
  • Globe EV: 20%, decided 2026-10-23 (evidence)

A feed can also give each price as drivers see it. Where that display price equals the price with no VAT added, although the feed states a VAT rate, adding the rate would show a price higher than the operator displays. On 23 October 2026 the repository owner decided that for each operator listed here, the VAT its feed states is read as already in its prices: each price is shown as published, including VAT, and says it was read as the operator's display price shows it.

Private and restricted chargers

The repository owner decided on 11 October 2026 that chargers marked as not for public use are hidden on the map unless a visitor ticks "Show chargers marked private or restricted" in the filters. OCPI 2.2.1 has no field that says a location is private, and no feed read so far marks one in any other field, so a location is marked only when the operator's own name for it contains one of these phrases, as whole words in any mix of capitals:

  • "private use", "private access" or "private only", as in "Private Use Only"
  • "private" at the end of the name or in brackets, as in "Site - PRIVATE"
  • "not public", "not for public use" or "non-public"
  • "no public access"
  • "restricted access" or "restricted use"
  • "staff only", "fleet only", "employees only", "residents only", "tenants only", "members only" or "permit holders only", with or without "use" before "only"
  • "staff car park"

Words that often name a place rather than a rule, such as "Fleet" in Fleetwood Road, "Depot", "Residential", "Visitor Centre" or "Blue Badge holders only", are not counted. A marked location is published unchanged: its details say which words were found, and the feed health page lists every location marked in the latest run. See the feed health page.

Separately, where the owner has decided so for an operator (see Sources above), a location where the operator marks every charge point for taxis only is hidden in the same way, and each such charge point is labelled "Taxis only".
