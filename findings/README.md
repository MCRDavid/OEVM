# Findings

Notes from building this map: what it takes to collect the open data that UK charge point
operators publish under the Public Charge Point Regulations 2023, and what we find in it. The
newest post comes first.

Each post says what was checked and when, and links to its sources. The posts describe what we
found; they do not say whether any operator meets the regulations, which is for the enforcement
authority to decide. Anything that is the project owner's opinion rather than a finding is
labelled as such.

The [transparency page](transparency/) has the dated record for each network, and the
[feed health page](status/) shows how each feed did in the latest daily run. If something here
is wrong or out of date, please [report a mistake](report/).
